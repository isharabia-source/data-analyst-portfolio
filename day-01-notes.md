The six steps (pretend store: a coffee shop) 
1. Ask: Why did sales drop in March? 
2. Prepare: Collect daily sales data from the register system. 
3. Process: Remove duplicate orders and fix missing dates. 
4. Analyze: Compare March sales to February by product and by day. 
5. Share: Show the owner a chart and a short summary. 
6. Act: The owner changes the menu and we check if sales recover.
